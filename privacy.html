Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

This website provides product information and links visitors to WhatsApp. When you choose to contact us, the information you send is handled through WhatsApp and is also subject to WhatsApp’s privacy terms.

Information you choose to share

You may choose to send your name, phone number, location, questions, symptoms, scan or test result, and delivery details. Please share only information you are comfortable sending. Do not send emergency medical information through this website.

Analytics

The site uses Meta Pixel, Microsoft Clarity and Contentsquare to understand visits and interactions, such as page views, scroll depth and WhatsApp button clicks. These services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

How information is used

Information you send may be used to respond to questions, explain available products, process an order, arrange delivery and provide customer support.

Your choices

You can browse the page without sending a WhatsApp message. You can also limit cookies through your browser settings. To ask about information you previously shared, contact us on WhatsApp.
