Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I know which package is suitable for me?

Message us with your main concerns and any scan information you already have. We can explain the listed product options so you can make an informed choice. This conversation is not a medical diagnosis.

Can I send my scan on WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the “Send Your Scan / Test Result” button and attach the result in WhatsApp. Share only information you are comfortable sending.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes, nationwide delivery is available. Send your state and location on WhatsApp so the current delivery details can be confirmed.

Is payment on delivery available?

Payment on delivery is available for eligible locations. Please confirm availability and delivery terms for your address before dispatch.

How long does delivery take?

Timing depends on your location and the courier. We will confirm the current estimate with you on WhatsApp before dispatch.

Can I speak with someone before ordering?

Yes. The website is designed to help you start a private WhatsApp conversation before making a purchase.

What information should I send on WhatsApp?

You can share your main symptoms, how long you have experienced them, your location and a scan or test result if available. For diagnosis, prescriptions or urgent symptoms, contact a qualified healthcare professional.
